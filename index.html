Validation Tool

Validation Tool is a personal, internal application used by its owner to test demand for product ideas. It creates small Google Ads Search campaigns in the owner's own Google Ads account, points them to landing pages, and reports clicks, spend and signups. It enforces a fixed spend cap per test and pauses campaigns automatically.

It is not offered to third parties and does not manage anyone else's Google Ads account.
